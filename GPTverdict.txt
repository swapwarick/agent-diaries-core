Final Verdict

As an engineering project, I think @agent-diaries/core is one of the more thoughtfully designed open-source agent coordination libraries I've seen in this space because it tackles a concrete systems problem—coordinating concurrent agents while avoiding redundant work—and backs its behavior with substantial telemetry and validation.

As an open-source product, I think its biggest gap is not the implementation. It's helping developers immediately understand why they need it, how it differs from Redis, Temporal, LangGraph, or AutoGen, and showing that through memorable APIs and compelling real-world examples rather than benchmark sophistication alone. If you close that gap, the technical foundation you've built is strong enough to support it.
